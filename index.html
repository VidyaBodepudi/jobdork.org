Why Direct ATS Dorking Outperforms Job Boards
👻

Eliminate Ghost Jobs

Third-party job boards leave expired posts up for weeks to harvest data and sell subscriptions. When you query Ashby, Greenhouse, or Lever directly, you only see jobs currently live in the company's applicant tracking system.

📥

Land in the Hiring Dashboard

Applying through external job board "Easy Apply" links dumps your resume into a messy third-party portal. Hitting the ATS board directly routes your application straight into the recruiter's internal screening queue.

⏱️

Be in the First 25 Applicants

Filtering by posts from the last 24 hours to 7 days gives you a massive tactical advantage. You reach hiring managers before the role gets cross-posted and flooded with hundreds of automated bot applications.

💡 Pro Tips: Advanced Boolean Dorking Tricks
Filter by Salary Range Add a number range with two periods to surface jobs with posted pay bands:
"$150,000..$220,000"
Target Specific Tech Stacks Include your core frameworks in the role input with exact quotes:
"Software Engineer" "Go" ("Kubernetes" OR "AWS")
Exclude Offshore Postings If you only want North American remote roles, append country constraints:
-"EMEA" -"LATAM" -"India" "US Only"
Keyboard Navigation Press Enter from any text field to trigger a search. Press Cmd+K / Ctrl+K to refocus the role input.
